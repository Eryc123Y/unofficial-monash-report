Question 3

5 marks

State the worst-case time complexity of A* with the zero heuristic on a graph with V nodes and E edges.

With h = 0 the algorithm is Dijkstra's. Each edge causes at most one push, so the heap holds at most E entries and every push or pop costs O(log E) = O(log V). The total is O((V + E) log V).
